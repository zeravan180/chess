AG Classic Games

Privacy Policy · سیاسەتا تایبەتمەندیێ · سياسة الخصوصية — Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game AG Classic Games (chess, domino, snakes & ladders, backgammon, draughts (dama), ludo and the "Majlis" voice/chat rooms; website zeravan180.github.io/chess and its Android app) collects, why, and how you can delete it. The game is made by an individual developer (Zeravan), contact: zeravan180@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID, provided by Google when you sign in.
  • Profile you choose: display name and photo (visible to other players); optional gender and birthday (visible only to you).
  • Game data: games and moves, scores, wins/losses, level and XP, coins and AG balance, items you own, gifts sent and received, and your room memberships.
  • Messages and voice: text messages and short voice messages you send in rooms and games, room names, descriptions and cover photos you upload. Live voice on room seats is sent directly between devices or through a voice relay server and is not recorded or stored.
  • Buying and selling AG: when you buy AG, or ask to sell AG back for Iraqi dinars, we keep a record of the request: the amount, the payment method you choose (for example FIB), the account number and name you enter for the transfer, the date and its status. We use this only to complete the transfer and to prevent fraud. We never receive your card details or bank password.
  • Friends: your player ID, friend requests you send and receive, and your friends list.
  • Safety: reports you send, people you block, and account suspensions.
  • Activity: online status, the time of your last visit and the number of visits, used for the game's statistics.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising ID, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

2. How we use it

  • To run the games, matchmaking, rooms, chat and voice.
  • To show your name, photo, level and scores to other players (leaderboards, tables, rooms).
  • To keep your coins, AG, items and purchases in your account.
  • To keep players safe: review reports, remove content and suspend accounts that break the rules.

3. Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) stores the game data.
  • Payment providers you choose (for example FIB) receive the transfer you make to us or we make to you.
  • A voice relay service (Metered) may pass live room audio between players when a direct connection is not possible; it is not stored.
  • Other players see your public profile, messages and voice messages in the rooms and games you join.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

  • Voice messages during games: about 1 minute. Room messages: usually up to 3 days.
  • Account and game data: while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed within 30 days, except purchase and gift records that we may keep for accounting and fraud prevention.

5. Children

The game is for players aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by database security rules so that private data can only be read by its owner.

7. Delete your account and data

In the game: open ⚙ Settings → "🗑 Delete my account" and confirm.

Without the app: email zeravan180@gmail.com from the Google email you play with, with the subject "Delete my AG Classic Games account".

We delete your profile, game data, coins, AG, items, messages, blocks and reports within 30 days. AG and items cannot be recovered after deletion.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: zeravan180@gmail.com.
